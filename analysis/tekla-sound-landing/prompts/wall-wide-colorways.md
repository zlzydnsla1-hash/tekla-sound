# Wall Wide colorway cutout series

Create one isolated photorealistic Wall Wide product cutout with exact locked geometry, camera, scale, centerline, margins, pale-oak frame, and single recessed matte-black lower slot. Use a genuinely transparent alpha background.

Color states change the woven textile face only. Terracotta is the default/resting state:

- Terracotta `#C2513A` — primary/default
- Ivory `#E7E0D2`
- Beige `#B9A58F`
- Cobalt Blue `#2457C5`
- Deep Green `#173F35`

All five images must align for instant hover swapping. On pointer leave, restore Terracotta. Preserve weave relief and product geometry; avoid text, logos, cables, drivers, controls, extra slots, environment, crop, and CGI gloss.
